## 🌟 Overview

**Millka CV** is an end-to-end resume creation and ATS scoring platform built for job seekers. It pairs modern multi-layout resume templates with Google Gemini AI to craft high-impact achievements, quantify work history, suggest role-specific keywords, and instantly export production-ready A4 PDFs.

- **Frontend App**: [https://millkacv-frontend.onrender.com](https://millkacv-frontend.onrender.com)
- **Backend API**: [https://millkacv-backend.onrender.com](https://millkacv-backend.onrender.com)

---

## ✨ Features

- **Gemini AI Resume Generation**: Generate complete, polished resumes from your background and selected template with one click.
- **ATS Analyzer & Scoring**: Real-time evaluation of keyword density, quantifiable impact, and formatting with actionable improvement tips.
- **Dynamic Templates**: Multiple professional layouts (Modern Clean, Classic Serif, Teal Executive, Obsidian Pro, Azure Corporate, Crimson Creative, and more).
- **1-Click High-Res PDF Export**: Instant, client-side A4 PDF generation without clipping or formatting loss.
- **External CV Scanner**: Scan existing PDF or text resumes to detect missing industry skills.
